# Daylight Recipes

A DaylightUI plugin for WoW: Forever. A pane on the right of the profession window: DaylightUI's own, or DaylightMogul's while Mogul runs your professions.

## Recipe

For the recipe you pick:
- **Skill-up chance** as a bar and a percentage, with the recipe's orange, yellow, green and grey points and about how many crafts a point takes. Your Overtime talent is counted.
- **Materials**, each priced: from the Auction House through DaylightMogul, or the cheapest vendor you can buy from (your reputation discount and Bartering counted). The total, and the cost per skill point.
- **Value** of what it makes: on the Auction House (after the 5% cut), at a vendor, or disenchanted, with the profit or loss each way.
- **Disenchants into**: each material, how many at a time, how often, and what it's worth.

## Missing

Every recipe of the open profession you don't know yet, by the skill it needs (the ones you can learn now stand out). Click one to see where it comes from: trainers, vendors (with the price), drops (with the chance) and quests, each with the place. Click a place for a waypoint (TomTom when it's installed, the game's map pin otherwise). Only your faction's people and places are shown. Search, and "only the ones my skill allows" in the settings.

## Settings

DaylightUI's options, Plugins, Recipes: pane width, Auction House prices on or off and which price (market, lowest buyout, today's average), counting your profession talents, the missing list filter and TomTom.
