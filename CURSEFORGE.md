# Daylight Recipes

**A profession window that levels you up:** your recipes, a step-by-step Leveling Guide, a crafting queue, and buy lists at the vendor, the Auction House and the trainer, all in the DaylightUI look.

Requires **DaylightUI**. Works on its own: it keeps its own Auction House prices, and uses **DaylightMogul**'s when Mogul is loaded. With it installed you can turn ForeverRecipes off.

---

## The window
Open any profession and Daylight Recipes' window takes the place of the game's (and DaylightUI's or Mogul's).
- **Your professions on the left** (click one to open it), with your alts' below: see what each alt knows. Herbalism, Skinning and Fishing open their own page (skill, rank, where to train next) and a **Camping** entry lists every profession's camping items. Right-click a main profession to **unlearn** it.
- **Swap button** beside the X: switch to the game's profession window and back any time.
- **Recipes:** search, "Can make" and "Skill-ups" filters, folding categories and how many you can make. The picked recipe shows its skill-up chance, what its materials cost and what you hold of them (bags, bank, mail, alts), what it's worth, and Create / Create All / Queue.
- **Leveling Guide:** pick your goal (150, 225, 300) and what the route aims for (cheapest materials, or after vendoring, disenchanting or selling what you make). The step you're on comes as a card: what to make and how many, its materials, and Make / Queue / Show recipe. Then every next step, numbered, with when to train each rank, and the rods and tools you'll need made along the way.
- **Materials:** everything the route needs, what you hold, and what's left to buy.
- **Queue:** queue recipes from anywhere, then "Craft next".
- **Missing:** every recipe you don't know yet, where to get it (checked against QuestieDB), nearest first, with waypoints, including reputation recipes ("Nightclaw Druids: Friendly (Current Rep: Neutral)", in the game's reputation colors).
- `/recipes` opens it on your last profession, even away from a crafting station.

## Where you shop and train
- **Vendor:** your buy list under the vendor's window. Double-click to buy exactly what you need; what the vendor doesn't sell is grayed out.
- **Auction House:** the same list with the Auction House and vendor prices side by side, and which is cheaper. One click makes an Auctionator shopping list when Auctionator is installed.
- **Trainer:** "Recommended" beside the trainer: only the recipes your Leveling Guide needs, and your next rank.
- Each one folds away to a small tab.

## Enchanting
Drag a cheap item into the slot and level on it: enchant after enchant, with the game's "replace the enchant?" answered for you (Auto-replace, a switch).

## Around the game
Item tooltips (what uses a material, what your guides need of it, and who on your account knows a recipe; only for the professions this character has learned, and only this character's guides), and trainer reminders in chat when a skill-up opens new recipes or you reach your rank's cap.

---

## In your language
Daylight Recipes speaks German, French, Spanish, Russian, Korean and Simplified Chinese (machine drafts, corrections welcome; words the game already translates use the game's own wording), and shows recipe, item, profession, zone and quest names in your game's language.

---

## Settings
DaylightUI options > **Plugins > Recipes**. Prefer DaylightUI's or Mogul's own profession window? Pick **Docked pane** there for the old pane beside it. Window opacity follows DaylightUI's, or set your own there.
